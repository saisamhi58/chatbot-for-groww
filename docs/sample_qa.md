# Sample Q&A — Mutual Fund FAQ Chatbot

Below are example queries and the kind of fact-only answers the assistant returns.
(Answers were generated by the prototype using the 5 source URLs in `sources.md`.)

1. **Q:** What is the minimum SIP amount?
   **A:** The minimum SIP amount for most HDFC mutual fund schemes is ₹100.
   **Sources:** https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth, https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

2. **Q:** What is the ELSS lock-in period?
   **A:** HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a mandatory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment.
   **Sources:** https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

3. **Q:** What is the exit load for HDFC Large Cap Fund?
   **A:** Exit load is 1% if redeemed within 1 year; no exit load after 1 year.
   **Sources:** https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund-direct-growth

4. **Q:** What is the expense ratio of HDFC Small Cap Fund?
   **A:** The direct plan expense ratio is around 0.6-0.8% (updated periodically — see latest factsheet).
   **Sources:** https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-small-cap-fund-direct-growth

5. **Q:** What is the riskometer for HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund?
   **A:** It is typically rated Moderate to High risk — refer to the latest factsheet.
   **Sources:** https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth

6. **Q:** Which fund is best for me?
   **A:** I only provide facts and do not give investment advice. Please consult a SEBI-registered advisor.
   **Sources:** https://www.sebi.gov.in/

7. **Q:** Should I invest in ELSS for tax saving?
   **A:** Facts-only assistant — I cannot recommend investments. For tax rules, refer to the official ELSS page: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

8. **Q:** How do I download my capital gains statement?
   **A:** Log in to the HDFC MF portal or Groww app, go to My Investments > Statements > Capital Gains.
   **Sources:** https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund-direct-growth

9. **Q:** What is the benchmark for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?
   **A:** The benchmark is typically the NIFTY 500 TRI.
   **Sources:** https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-equity-fund-direct-growth

10. **Q:** What returns will I get if I invest ₹10,000?
    **A:** I cannot compute or predict returns. Please refer to the official factsheet for historical data.
    **Sources:** https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth
